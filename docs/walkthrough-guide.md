# Suggested 45-minute walkthrough
1. 5 min — explain requirements and assumptions.
2. 8 min — architecture and service ownership.
3. 10 min — demo claim creation, assignment, decision event, claim status update.
4. 7 min — show workload/reporting projections and eventual consistency.
5. 10 min — discuss reliability, Kafka delivery, outbox, idempotency, security, observability.
6. 5 min — questions and what you would build next.

Be precise: Kafka is implemented in source, but the end-to-end build/run must be verified locally before you claim it works.
